Mobile banking transfer.

A focused case about validating a high-trust mobile banking flow through prototypes, speed, usability, and repeated user testing.

Role

Senior UX Designer.

Product

Interactive prototype of a banking transfer tested with 25 users.

Scale

Designed for a bank with 2.5M+ clients in Poland.

Draft narrative

The product lesson

This case can stay concise and strong: a specific task, a high-trust environment, a prototype, and repeated usability evidence. It should show how UX decisions become safer when they are tested against real behavior.
